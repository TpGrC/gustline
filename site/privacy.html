Privacy policy

Windrova by Halewood Systems · Last updated 17 November 2026

The short version

Windrova has no accounts and no ads, and it doesn't track you. We don't collect, sell or share your personal data.

Your location

Windrova only uses your location if you tap the "my location" button, and your phone asks for permission first. The coordinates are used to show the forecast for where you are. They are sent to the forecast service (Open-Meteo) and to a place-name service (BigDataCloud) only to get that forecast and the name of the place. We don't store them.

Searches and tapped places

When you search for a place or tap the map, the place name or coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to find the place and get its forecast.

Services the app connects to

To show the map and forecasts, the app loads content from these providers. Like any website, they receive your device's IP address as part of the normal connection:

  • GitHub Pages (hosts Windrova and its weather data)
  • Open-Meteo (forecasts and place search)
  • BigDataCloud (place names)
  • Esri / ArcGIS Online (map background and terrain)
  • Google Fonts, unpkg and jsDelivr (fonts and map software)

Weather model data comes from NOAA (the US National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration).

Settings stored on your device

Your language, units, last map position and chosen layer are saved only on your own device so the app opens the way you left it. Clearing the app's data removes them.

Premium

Premium purchases, once available, will be handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Children

Windrova is a general-audience weather app and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the developer contact email shown on Windrova's Google Play page.
