Privacy policy

Windrova by Halewood Systems · Last updated 18 November 2026

The short version

Windrova has no accounts and no ads, and it doesn't track you. We don't collect, sell or share your personal data.

Your location

Windrova only uses your location if you tap the "my location" button, and your phone asks for permission first. The coordinates are used to show the forecast for where you are. They are sent to the forecast service (MET Norway) only to get that forecast. Place names are looked up inside the app from our own list. We don't store your location.

Searches and tapped places

When you search for a place, what you type is sent to Photon (an OpenStreetMap search service by komoot) to find matching places. When you tap the map, the coordinates are sent to MET Norway to get the forecast.

Services the app connects to

To show the map and forecasts, the app loads content from these providers. Like any website, they receive your device's IP address as part of the normal connection:

  • GitHub Pages (hosts Windrova and its weather data)
  • MET Norway, api.met.no (point forecasts)
  • Photon by komoot (place search, OpenStreetMap data)
  • EUMETSAT (satellite pictures, satellite rain, lightning and fire detection)
  • NASA GIBS (satellite pictures)
  • EMSC (earthquakes) and GDACS (tropical storms)
  • Windrova relay on Cloudflare (passes forecast requests to MET Norway; it sends only the map position, never anything about you)
  • Google Fonts, unpkg and jsDelivr (fonts and map software)

Weather model data comes from NOAA (the US National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) ECMWF (European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts, CC BY 4.0) and Environment and Climate Change Canada (GEM model). Point forecasts come from MET Norway (CC BY 4.0). Map shapes come from Natural Earth and place names from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0).

Settings stored on your device

Your language, units, last map position and chosen layer are saved only on your own device so the app opens the way you left it. Clearing the app's data removes them.

Premium

Premium purchases, once available, will be handled by our payment provider (Stripe) or by the app store. We never see your full card details.

Children

Windrova is a general-audience weather app and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
